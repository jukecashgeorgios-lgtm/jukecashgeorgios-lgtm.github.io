This Privacy Policy explains how JUKECASH handles information when you use our mobile application and related services. We respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about the technologies and third-party services used by the app.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

JUKECASH does not require users to create an account or maintain a personal profile on our own server.

Information entered or generated through the app may be stored locally on your device, including:

  • Workout records and exercise history.
  • Training programs and workout progress.
  • Nutrition logs, food entries and calorie information.
  • Running and cycling activity records.
  • Distances, durations and activity measurements.
  • Personal fitness goals and preferences.

These records are stored locally for the app's functionality. Their availability may depend on your device, backups and whether you uninstall the application.

3. Location Data and GPS

JUKECASH may access your device's location while using GPS-based running and cycling features.

Location information is used to support activity tracking, including routes, distance, speed and related measurements.

GPS access depends on the permissions you grant through your device settings. You can disable location access at any time, although GPS-based features may then be unavailable.

Activity and route records are stored locally on your device according to the app's current storage functionality.

4. Firebase and Google Analytics

The Android version of JUKECASH is connected to Google Analytics for Firebase.

This service may collect technical information and app usage events to help understand application usage and improve its performance.

Depending on the SDK configuration and device settings, information may include app interactions, device information, identifiers and diagnostic information.

The Android Analytics stream is currently configured in Firebase. Data collection depends on the SDK implementation and applicable consent settings.

We have not identified an iOS Analytics data stream in the Firebase project at the time this policy was written.

For more information, see: Google Privacy Policy .

5. Firebase Crashlytics

The Android version of JUKECASH uses Firebase Crashlytics to identify, monitor and diagnose application crashes and technical errors.

Crash reports may include technical information such as device model, operating system version, application version and details about the crash.

This information is used to improve application stability, identify technical problems and enhance the user experience.

Firebase Crashlytics is not currently used in the iOS version of JUKECASH.

Further information is available in Firebase Privacy and Security .

6. Food Barcode Scanner and Open Food Facts

JUKECASH includes a food barcode scanning feature that may use the Open Food Facts database to retrieve product information.

When a barcode is scanned, the barcode number may be sent to Open Food Facts to search for matching food product information, such as product name and nutritional values.

Open Food Facts is an independent third-party service and is subject to its own privacy policy.

More information: Open Food Facts Privacy Policy .

7. Advertising and Google AdMob

JUKECASH contains an integration for Google AdMob. However, at the time of this policy's publication, our AdMob account has not been approved and advertisements are not being served through the app.

If advertising services are activated in the future, advertising-related information may be processed by Google and its advertising partners, subject to applicable consent requirements and settings. This Privacy Policy will be updated where necessary.

More information: Google Advertising Policies .

8. Subscriptions and In-App Purchases

JUKECASH offers two Premium subscription options: a monthly subscription and an annual subscription. The annual subscription is offered at a discounted price compared to paying monthly.

Certain advanced workout programs, including Strength, Human Flag, Front Lever and Muscle-Up, are available through separate one-time purchases.

An active JUKECASH Premium subscription is required to access and use these advanced programs. The one-time purchase does not replace or include the Premium subscription.

One-time purchases are charged once and do not renew automatically. Premium subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled through the relevant application store.

All subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play, depending on the platform used.

JUKECASH does not directly collect or store users' full payment card details. Payment information is handled by the respective platform according to its privacy policy.

For further information, please refer to:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy
